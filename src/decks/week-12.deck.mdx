# Flatbreads

Week 12. Turn the oven off.

---

## Conduction, not hot air

| Bread | Heated by | Temperature | Time |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Tin loaf | oven air | 230 °C | 35 min |
| Pita | steel or stone | 250–300 °C | 2–4 min |
| Roti | tawa, then flame | 230 °C | 90 s |
| Naan | tandoor wall | 400–480 °C | 60–90 s |

Metal moves heat into dough far faster than air does. So: hotter, thinner,
faster, and barely any water lost.

---

## The pocket, in three steps

- both faces pass 100 °C and set into gas-tight skins
- the wet middle then boils
- steam expands **1600×** in volume, faster than it can escape, and the skins part

Yeast contributes flavour. Steam does the lifting.

# The pocket is in the rolling, not the recipe

---

## Three ways to lose it

- **uneven disc** — steam vents through the soft half, half a pocket
- **too thick** — the middle never reaches 100 °C in time
- **surface under 220 °C** — it dries before it can flash, and you have a cracker

A roti has no leavening at all and still balloons in four seconds.

---

## Naan: week 9, flattened

Fat shortens the gluten. Yoghurt adds four things at once:

- water — it is about 85% water
- fat and milk solids
- lactose and milk protein, so it browns darker
- lactic acid to pH 4.5, which slackens the gluten and tenderises

Tandoor wall at 400–480 °C, wet side down, ninety seconds.

---

## Masa has no protein network

Maize simmered and steeped in **1% calcium hydroxide**, around pH 12:

- pericarp dissolved, starch partly gelatinised → a dough that coheres
- niacin freed from niacytin → no pellagra

A corn tortilla is held together by gelatinised starch and calcium. A flour
tortilla is gluten kept short with fat — which is why only one of them folds
cold.

---

## What held up each bread

- **1–5** gluten, argued over with water
- **6** the same network, a different population
- **7** pentosan gel, defended by acid
- **8** a gelatinised skin, boiled on
- **9–10** gluten deliberately compromised, with fat and with butter
- **11** a chemical reaction instead of an organism
- **12** steam, and nothing else

Identify the mechanism and the formula reads itself.
