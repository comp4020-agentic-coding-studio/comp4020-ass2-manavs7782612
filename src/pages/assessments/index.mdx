Three bake-offs, and every one is judged the same way: bread baked at home
(or wherever you can get an oven) and carried in whole and uncut, then cut
open on the bench in front of Marisol or Idris while you answer questions
about the crumb. The first covers weeks 1 to 4, the second weeks 5 to 8, and
the third is a bread of your own design covering the whole semester, proposed
at Bake 11 and baked after teaching ends.
