Each lecture runs the same day as that week's Bake and does the thinking the
bench has no time for: why 65% hydration behaves the way it does, what
gluten actually is, why a levain finishes faster at 29 °C than at 24 °C. The
Bake then tests it — you carry the mechanism straight from the lecture into
the dough. Every week has a slide deck, linked from its lecture page.
