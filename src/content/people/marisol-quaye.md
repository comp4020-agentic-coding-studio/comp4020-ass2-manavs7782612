## A market stall before a lecture theatre

Marisol ran a stall at the Sunday market for eleven years before this course
existed — six sourdough variants and a dark rye that always sold out first —
and she built SLOP3422 in the order she wishes someone had taught her: a dough
you can actually control before a wild culture that will not wait for you. She
teaches six of the twelve Bakes: the plain loaf, the baguette and pizza on
commercial yeast, then sourdough, rye and flatbreads once a starter and a hot
dry surface are the two things left to learn.

She judges the showpiece bake-off alone in week 12, and sits with Idris to cut
and question both loaves at the lean-dough bake-off in week 4. Formulas and
schedules for every week she teaches are on the [Bake pages](/sessions/); the
[bake-offs](/assessments/) themselves carry their own briefs and marking.

## Who to ask, and when

Start with Idris for anything happening to the dough right now — a slack
ciabatta, a levain that will not double, a bake going wrong at 7am on a bench
morning. That is what he is there for, and it is usually faster solved in
front of the dough than described in an email. Email Marisol instead for the
course-level questions: marks, extensions, the order of the weeks, or anything
about the six Bakes she runs directly. She reads email most days during
teaching weeks, and there is no separate consultation hour beyond the bench
session itself — if a question needs a room, the next Monday has one.
